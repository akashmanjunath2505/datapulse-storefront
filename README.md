## ⚡ Quick Benchmark Takeaways (2026 Data)

1. **The $19–$49/mo "Credibility Sweet Spot":**
   B2B SaaS products priced between $19 and $49/mo convert **2.3× higher** from trial to paid than products priced under $10/mo, while suffering **40% lower monthly churn**. Price signals reliability to business buyers.
2. **The Churn Curve by Entry Price:**
   - **$1 – $9/mo tier:** 14.2% average monthly churn
   - **$10 – $29/mo tier:** 7.8% average monthly churn
   - **$30 – $99/mo tier:** 5.1% average monthly churn
   - **$100+/mo tier:** 3.2% average monthly churn
3. **Annual Billing Advantage:**
   Offering a 2-month discount (16.7%) converts 22% to 35% of all new subscribers upfront, dramatically lowering blended churn and supplying upfront cash flow.
4. **The Price Increase Reality:**
   Founders who raised prices by 20% to 50% lost an average of only **3%** of their customer base, resulting in net positive MRR in **94%** of cases.
